Last updated: June 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Na Liang, an individual based in Bavaria, Germany, collects, uses, stores, shares, and protects personal information in connection with the Audio Speed Changer mobile application and related support, analytics, diagnostics, and feedback activities.

Audio Speed Changer allows users to import audio and video files, modify pitch and speed, and export the resulting files. The App is distributed through the Apple App Store and is intended for global availability.

A key privacy point is that audio and video files selected by users are processed locally on the user's device. We do not upload, collect, review, store, sell, or share the contents of those audio or video files through our own servers.

3. Local audio and video processing

When you import audio or video files into the App, those files are used so the App can perform the functions requested by you, including speed adjustment, pitch adjustment, preview, and export.

The contents of your selected audio and video files are processed locally on your device. This means the media content itself is not transmitted to us, uploaded to our servers, stored by us, or used by us for analytics, advertising, product development, artificial intelligence training, or any other separate purpose.

Exported files are created and handled according to your own actions, your device settings, and the locations or apps you choose when saving or sharing the exported file. We are not responsible for third-party apps, storage locations, or sharing destinations that you select outside Audio Speed Changer.

Although media file contents are not uploaded to us, technical information about how the App is used may still be collected, such as export counts, app events, crash information, device identifiers, IP address, and analytics data as explained below.
